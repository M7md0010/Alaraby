Packaging Engineering & DFM

How We Reuse 100% of the Television Carton

A 55" Tornado TV carton has two massive flat faces. Instead of leaving 90% of the cardboard as waste or weakening the outer box with perforations, we engineered a Two-Face Hybrid System printed on the inner liner with 1-color water-based Flexo ink.

FACE 1 • 50% OF CARTON
Pre-Planned Core Family Utility

All-in-One Remote Stand & Perpetual Calendar Hub

Every TV buyer immediately needs a place for their new TV remote, receiver remote, AC remote, phone, and daily calendar. Face 1 has pre-printed interior guidelines that fold in 60 seconds into our flagship multi-compartment desk & coffee-table organizer.

FACE 2 • 50% OF CARTON
Universal Dot-and-Square Coordinate Grid

QR-Guided "Choose Your Design" Modular Platform

Instead of forcing one shape on every household, Face 2 is printed only with a clean universal grid of small squares and dots. Customers scan the box QR code, pick their favorite lifestyle product on this website, and use the grid squares to cut and slot small pieces together!

STEP 1 • CUT ON GRID

Cut Pieces A, B & C Along Solid Grid Lines

STEP 2 • FOLD & SLOT

Interlock Side Walls & Controller Cradles

STEP 3 • LOCK TOWER & LOAD

Mount T-Tower & Place Controllers + Headset

Slide the vertical T-shaped headphone hook (Piece C) down into the top rear bridge slot and place your 2 PlayStation controllers and headset!

Egyptian Home Decoration

Trio of Small Stepped Geometric Pyramids (3-Piece Set)

FINISHED 3D PRODUCT RESULT (3 PYRAMIDS)
2D SQUARE-GRID & 3-STEP BLUEPRINT
STEP 1 • CUT NESTED SQUARES

Cut Concentric Grid Squares & Center Cross-Slots

Cut out the nested square plates (Piece A: 7×7, 6×6, 5×5 down to 1×1 squares) and punch the small + cross-slit at the center dot of each square.

STEP 2 • SLOT CENTER SPINES

Interlock Pairs of Triangular Spines at 90°

Cut out the 3 pairs of notched triangular spine supports (Piece B) and slide each pair together at a 90-degree angle to form three sturdy 3D cross-bases.

STEP 3 • STACK PYRAMID TRIO

Slide Squares Down Spines Largest to Smallest

Stack the square layers down each vertical spine from largest at the bottom to smallest at the top peak to complete your 3-pyramid sculpture set!

Living Room Utility

Geometric Faceted Tissue Box Cover

FINISHED 3D PRODUCT RESULT
2D SQUARE-GRID & 3-STEP BLUEPRINT
STEP 1 • CUT UNFOLDED NET

Cut Outer Cross Perimeter & Top Tissue Slot

Cut along the straight outer grid lines of Piece A (18×14 squares unfolded box net) including the 4 side locking tabs (Piece B), and pop out the central 6×2 square tissue opening.

STEP 2 • FOLD DIAGONALS

Pre-Fold Straight & Diagonal Facet Lines

Using a ruler edge connecting the printed grid dots, crease the 4 main 90-degree top edges and the diagonal corner lines to create the 3D diamond-faceted side walls.

STEP 3 • LOCK TABS & COVER

Tuck Side Locking Tabs into Corner Slits

Bring the four walls upright, tuck the 4 projecting side tabs (Piece B) into the matching corner slits, and place the finished cover directly over your tissue box!
